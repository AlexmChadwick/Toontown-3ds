## Open Toontown (BSD 3-Clause)

Parts of this project are derived from Open Toontown
(<https://github.com/open-toontown/open-toontown>):

- The toon colour table in `source/render/ui.cpp`, from `toontown/toon/ToonDNA.py`.
- The generated DC class/field numbers and hash in `source/net/dc_generated.h`,
  computed from `etc/otp.dc` and `etc/toon.dc`.
- Gameplay constants such as toon movement speeds, from
  `otp/otpbase/OTPGlobals.py`.
- The SpeedChat menu tree and phrase table in `source/ui/speedchat_data.*`, generated by
  `tools/gen_speedchat.py` from `toontown/chat/TTChatInputSpeedChat.py` and the
  localizer files.
- Protocol and asset-layout knowledge documented in `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design/`.

## Other references

- **DNA assembly.** `tools/pipeline/assemble.py` reimplements the placement rules of
  Disney's `libtoontown` DNA loader. It was written from reading that code, not copied
  from it.
- **Projection test.** `source/ui/test/projection_test.cpp` reimplements citro3d's
  matrix helpers (zlib license) for a host-side test.
