## Open Toontown (BSD 3-Clause)

Parts of this project are derived from Open Toontown
(<https://github.com/open-toontown/open-toontown>):

- The toon colour table in `source/render/ui.cpp`, from `toontown/toon/ToonDNA.py`.
- The generated DC class/field numbers and hash in `source/net/dc_generated.h`,
  computed from `etc/otp.dc` and `etc/toon.dc`.
- Gameplay constants such as toon movement speeds, from
  `otp/otpbase/OTPGlobals.py`.
- The SpeedChat menu tree and phrase table in `source/ui/speedchat_data.*`, generated by
  `tools/gen_speedchat.py` from `toontown/chat/TTChatInputSpeedChat.py` and the
  localizer files.
- Protocol and asset-layout knowledge documented in `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design/`.

## Other references

- **DNA assembly.** `tools/pipeline/assemble.py` reimplements the placement rules of
  Disney's `libtoontown` DNA loader. It was written from reading that code, not copied
  from it.
- **Suit paths.** `source/world/suit_path.cpp` reimplements libtoontown's
  `DNAStorage::get_suit_path_breadth_first` and `SuitLegList` (read from the Open Toontown
  Panda3D fork's sources, not copied) and is checked against that library by
  `tools/suit_test`. The cog name/level/HP/height table `source/world/suit_tables.h` is
  generated by `tools/gen_suit_tables.py` from `SuitDNA.py`, `SuitBattleGlobals.py`,
  `TTLocalizerEnglish.py` and `Suit.py`.
- **Projection test.** `source/ui/test/projection_test.cpp` reimplements citro3d's
  matrix helpers (zlib license) for a host-side test.

## Boss implementation and offline metadata

The VP phase/attack constants, pie charge and trajectory formulas, cage/ramp
behavior, barrier protocol and NPC DNA metadata in `source/world/boss_logic.*`,
`source/world/boss_battle.*`, `source/net/client_boss.cpp` and
`tools/pipeline/bake_boss.py` are derived from the Open Toontown files cited in
`docs/design/bosses.md`; the Open Toontown BSD notice above applies. The ignored
`sdmc-out/3ds/toontown3ds/data/boss/` models, textures, animation and collision are
converted Toontown game assets and remain outside this project's MIT license.
No upstream source files are vendored by this work.

CFO crane placement/control limits, object state and impact semantics, goon radar/
stun/grab behavior, Resistance toon DNA and train timings in `source/world/cfo_*`,
`source/net/cfo_state.cpp`, `source/net/client_cfo.cpp` and the extended boss baker
are derived from the pinned Open Toontown sources listed in `docs/design/bosses.md`.
The Open Toontown BSD notice above applies. CFO baked assets, including the stock
NPC DNA metadata, remain converted game assets outside the MIT license.

CJ jury/cannon ballistics, chair placement, gavel timing/heading tables, scale
pivots/tilt, lawyer evidence/stun timing, barrier/reward semantics and NPC DNA in
`source/world/cj_*`, `source/net/cj_state.*`, `source/net/client_cj.cpp`, the boss
protocol blocks and `tools/pipeline/bake_cj.py` derive from the pinned Open Toontown
sources cited in `docs/design/bosses.md`. The Open Toontown BSD notice above applies.
Converted CJ game models/textures/animation/collision and DNA metadata remain
outside the MIT license; no upstream source files are vendored.

## Cogdominiums

The native Cogdo protocol, maze layout/walking/drop algorithms and offline
metadata tools derive from the pinned Open Toontown cogdominium, MazeBase,
MazeSuit and NPCToons specifications cited in `docs/design/cogdo.md`. The Open
Toontown BSD 3-Clause notice above applies. The committed Cogdo SD kit under
`sdmc-out/3ds/toontown3ds/data/cogdo/` contains Disney-derived models, textures,
collision and ToonDNA metadata. Like the RomFS assets, it is excluded from this
project's MIT license; no Disney asset license is granted.

The offline baker optionally imports the separately installed stock
`fast-simplification==0.2.0` wheel (MIT); its code is not vendored or linked into
the 3DS client. It is installed in ignored worktree-local tooling scratch.
